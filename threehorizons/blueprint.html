Horizon One — Status Quo

Congress is trying to govern in the 21st century with processes rooted in the 19th century and technology dating back to the 20th century.

The institution is falling behind and, unless its trajectory changes, risks becoming irrelevant.

Horizon Two Minus

Over the last decade, Congressional modernization initiatives have eased immediate pressures created by Members and staff calling for updates to Congressional operations.

Though progress has been made, Congress has not adopted conditions or processes to achieve lasting, sustaining improvement.
